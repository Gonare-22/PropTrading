Stoch RSI Entry Permission Logic

Update only the Stoch RSI entry-permission logic. Do not change the existing stop-loss, profit-protection, entry, or exit rules.

- Long permission: When Stoch RSI K crosses/reaches 80 or above, activate long-entry permission.
- Keep long-entry permission active while Stoch RSI K remains above 50.
- When Stoch RSI K crosses below 50, deactivate long-entry permission.
- Short permission: When Stoch RSI K crosses/reaches 20 or below, activate short-entry permission.
- Keep short-entry permission active while Stoch RSI K remains below 50.
- When Stoch RSI K crosses above 50, deactivate short-entry permission.
- The 20/50 EMA crossover remains the actual entry trigger.
- Stoch RSI permission only determines whether a new long or short entry is allowed.
- Do not use Stoch RSI crossing 50 as an exit signal for an existing position.
