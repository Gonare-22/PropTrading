📊 How to Manually Verify Trades

Step-by-Step Manual Testing Guide

Step 1: Run a Backtest

  • Select: Nifty 50
  • Date Range: 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  • Interval: 1 Day
  • Risk: 5% (for realistic results)
  • Click: Run Backtest

Step 2: Pick a Trade from the Table

Example: Trade #1

  • Signal Time: 2024-01-15 (when EMAs crossed)
  • Entry Time: 2024-01-16 (next day)
  • Entry Price: 21,450.50 (actual execution price)

Step 3: Verify on TradingView or Yahoo Finance

  1. Go to Yahoo Finance - Nifty 50 Historical Data
  2. Find the Signal Date (e.g., Jan 15, 2024)
  3. Check the Close price on that day
  4. Find the Entry Date (next day - Jan 16, 2024)
  5. Check the Open price on that day → This should match Entry Price!

Step 4: Verify EMAs (Optional - Use TradingView)

  1. Go to TradingView
  2. Search for NIFTY
  3. Add indicators: EMA 20, EMA 50, EMA 100
  4. Navigate to the Signal Date
  5. Check if EMA50 crossed EMA100 on that day
  6. Verify trade was executed at next day's OPEN

Understanding the Trade Table

Column What It Means
Signal Time When EMAs crossed (end of this bar)
Entry Time Next bar when trade executed (at OPEN price)
Entry Price Actual strike price - The OPEN of Entry Time bar
Exit Signal Time When exit signal triggered (EMA20 crossed EMA50)
Exit Time Next bar when exit executed (at OPEN price)
Position Size Number of shares/units bought
PnL (₹) Position Size × (Exit Price - Entry Price)
Equity After Running balance after this trade

How the strategy works

Entry signals:

  • Bullish (LONG): EMA50 crosses above EMA100 → Enter at next bar OPEN
  • Bearish (SHORT): EMA50 crosses below EMA100 → Enter at next bar OPEN

Exit signals:

  • Long exit: EMA20 crosses below EMA50 → Exit at next bar OPEN
  • Short exit: EMA20 crosses above EMA50 → Exit at next bar OPEN

Trade execution: Signal detected at bar N close → Trade executed at bar N+1 open. This uses the actual strike price (open price of next bar) rather than indicator values, avoiding look-ahead bias.

Risk management: The "Risk Per Trade %" controls how much capital is used per trade:

  • 100% = Full capital (aggressive, higher returns/losses)
  • 20% = 20% of current equity per trade (moderate)
  • 5% = 5% of current equity per trade (conservative, realistic)
  • 3% = Professional risk management standard

Trade sizing: Position size = (Equity × Risk%) / Entry Price. This dynamically adjusts based on available capital.

Trade log details:

  • Signal Time: The timestamp when EMA crossover detected
  • Entry/Exit Time: The next bar's timestamp where trade executed
  • Entry/Exit Price: The actual strike price (open price) at execution
  • PnL: Absolute profit/loss in currency
  • PnL %: Profit/loss as percentage of initial capital
  • Equity After: Running equity balance after trade closes

Note: Actual broker margin and risk sizing are not simulated. The model uses the reported initial capital only for signal sizing and P&L calculation.
